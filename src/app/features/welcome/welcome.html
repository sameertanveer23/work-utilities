Dev Utilities

Small tools, minimal clicks. Press Ctrl K to jump straight to one.

A personal toolbox for the small, repetitive jobs that interrupt real work — pasting a list of IDs into a SQL query, naming a branch, decoding a token, recoloring an icon. Each tool does one thing, updates as you type, and puts the result one click from your clipboard.

  • Keyboard first CtrlK to switch tools, CtrlEnter to copy.
  • Nothing leaves your browser No backend, no analytics, no network calls. It works offline.
  • Bookmarkable Every tool has its own URL. Star the ones you live in.
